# Constant-size limb source prompts

Built-in image generation. The accepted base/working image is a style reference. Each texture is cropped and resized once to its native dimensions, then rotated and translated at unit scale. Pose changes come from elbow/wrist articulation rather than fitting whole arms to destinations.

## upper

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one isolated pixel-art UPPER-ARM SLEEVE segment for a 2D articulated character, on a genuinely transparent background. Reference: approved cyberpunk decker's dark jacket and pixel palette only. Generate ONE straight, SHORT horizontal sleeve tube from LEFT shoulder attachment to RIGHT elbow joint. It will be mechanically cropped ONCE to THIRTEEN pixels wide by FIVE pixels high, with pivot centers at the middle of the left and right ends. These dimensions must feel slender and consistent. Dark navy/black-violet cloth, crisp black outline, just one narrow muted violet highlight and sparse folds. No big decorative purple band, no loud cyan patches. Rounded enclosed attachment caps at both ends so two segments can overlap seamlessly. Broad hard native pixel blocks, very little detail. Horizontal silhouette about 13:5. The piece rotates without changing scale; do not draw a bend or foreshortening. Draw it large, centered and isolated. No person, torso, hand, fingers, head, visor, room, desk, other arm, separate parts, labels, text, letters, numbers, multiple views, background color or checkerboard. This is a quiet far-arm jacket segment, behind the closer arm, matching the reference.

## hand

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: one isolated pixel-art hand making a small pinch/grip, with genuinely transparent background. Reference: the approved cyberpunk decker's adult warm tan skin, strong black hand outlines and 64x64 pixel style. Generate ONLY ONE compact hand, upright with the wrist at BOTTOM CENTER, and a small thumb and curved index finger grasping toward the LEFT near the upper half. The left fingertips will pinch the right edge of the character's forehead visor. It will be mechanically cropped ONCE to SEVEN pixels wide by SEVEN pixels high and then only translated/rotated at constant size. Show a simple believable adult palm and bent fingers with black outline and just two or three tan/orange shades, broad hard pixels, minimal detail. Avoid an oversized fist, long fingers, huge knuckle details, foreshortening, open palm, pointing gesture or spread fingers. A compact simple grip, silhouette about 1:1. Slightly open thumb/index pinch at LEFT, remaining fingers naturally curled in the palm, wrist connection at bottom middle. The palm must be large enough to read on an LED display. One hand large and centered on transparent canvas. No arm, sleeve, head, body, face, visor, ring, jewelry, glove, room, text, letters, numbers, multiple hands, separate pieces, background color or checkerboard.
